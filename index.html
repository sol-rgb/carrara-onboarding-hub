The team

The people you'll build with.

There were a handful of us in 2024, held together by an interest in helping however our skills and energy fit. Mostly companies bringing us in to recruit, with some finance and operations on the side.

That December we launched as Carrara with a small party. We couldn’t fully explain what we did yet, but we knew we wanted to “find and form exception”, whatever it turned out to look like.

We started 2025 with three priorities. They are still a fair description of what good looks like here:

  • Upgrade our quality from a B to an A;
  • Build systems, so we knew how we were adding value and could set expectations and exceed them; and
  • Get more and better clients, with the goal of doubling revenue by January 2026.

Midway through the year it began to crystallize. We could be far more selective about who we worked with. Existing clients expanded, and new work kept arriving by referral and word of mouth. It still does, which is why you will not find a sales team. Our mix evened out, from recruiting-heavy to talent, finance, expansion and special situations, and the lines between them began to blur. They still blur; that is the job.

That December the whole team drove up to a lodge on Kenoza Lake, in upstate New York, for three days: 32 people by then, more than double where the year started. We went up wanting to play on a bigger stage.

We did play on a bigger stage. By the end of 2026 the work had changed shape in four ways:

1/ New games

We brought on new marquee clients this year, including Anthropic, OpenAI Foundation, Amazon, Hertz and Polymarket.

2/ Expanded games

Existing relationships grew: 18 of our 54 clients expanded their work with us in 2026. We’ve been working with companies like Brave Health, Modal and January for over 2 years now.

3/ Winning games

The outcomes we delivered were larger in scope and impact than anything we had done before.

4/ Team games

The team grew alongside the work, from 32 people at Kenoza Lake to nearly 60 in Mexico City. You come in after that, which is further along than most of the people in these photographs were when they started.

Email signature

Set your Carrara signature.

You do this once, in your Carrara Gmail. The signature lives in a Google Doc template: copy it out, then change the name and the role to yours.

  1. Open the email signature template and copy the signature.
  2. Go to your Carrara Gmail inbox.
  3. Click the Settings icon, top right, then See all settings.
  4. Scroll down to Signature and click Create new. Name it Carrara.
  5. Paste the signature you copied from the template.
  6. Change the text to your own name and role.
  7. Under Signature defaults, set For new emails use and On reply/forward use to Carrara.
  8. Scroll to the bottom and click Save changes.

Time-Off & Holiday Policy

At Carrara, we operate at a high level; which means we also need time to reset. Great work comes from people who are energized, not running on fumes.

Overview

Our Time-Off Policy is built on trust, responsibility, and impact. We expect everyone to be thoughtful about balancing personal time with client and team needs. That means:

  • Taking time off proactively, not just when you're burned out.
  • Aiming for at least two days off per quarter (with prior approval) to recharge.
  • Communicating early and clearly so we can plan around time off.

Taking time off is part of sustaining high performance. We do our best work when we take care of ourselves and each other.

Scope

This policy applies to all Carrara team members, both full-time and part-time. The key areas covered are time off and holiday guidance.

Taking time off

Purpose and philosophy

We recognize the importance of stepping away from work to rest, recharge, and focus on what matters most. Our approach to time off is flexible and built on trust, ensuring team members take care of themselves while continuing to meet client and team commitments.

Guidelines

  • Plan in advance: for vacation, personal days, or other planned time off, give as much notice as possible, ideally at least two weeks. Team members are responsible for load balancing and ensuring outcomes during their time off.
  • Be mindful of team and client commitments: consider upcoming deadlines, projects, and client needs, and collaborate with the team so there is no significant impact to key deliverables.
  • Balance personal and business needs: pace time off in a way that aligns with both personal well-being and team goals.

Requesting time off

All time off requests require manager approval to ensure smooth operations. Once approved:

  • Coordinate with your manager and/or team to confirm timing.
  • Post in #g-ooo using the format below.
FormatYour Name - OOO (Start Date - End Date)(Optional) Give a short explanation of your time away.
ExampleEric - OOO (01/01/2026 - 01/05/2026)Out of town visiting my grandparents.

Unplanned absences

If an unplanned event arises, such as a personal emergency or illness, please inform your manager/team as soon as possible. Notify them promptly with a quick message or email, and provide updates as needed if additional days off are required.

Holidays

Carrara operates in a dynamic environment where delivering exceptional work is our priority, but we also value time off. We trust our team to take time off in a way that supports both personal well-being and business success. We recognize that different holidays hold significance for different people, and encourage team members to take time off for what matters most to them.

End of year time off considerations

  • Between Christmas and New Year's: work is typically slower for Carrara. While some client check-ins may be necessary, we encourage the team to unplug and recharge as much as possible.
  • Between Thanksgiving and Christmas: tends to be an all-hands-on-deck time as we support clients in wrapping up the year. We ask team members to be mindful and limit time off during this period to ensure we're available when it matters most.

FAQs

How does time off work for team members on contract?

Contracted team members should refer to their agreement for details on time off. In most instances, contractors should be able to take time off and deduct it from hours worked that week.

Who should I contact with questions about time off?

Speak with your manager, or reach out to Eric Fernandez or Kenna Meyerhoff with additional questions.

What if I need to take time off unexpectedly for a personal emergency?

We expect life to happen, and want team members to be able to take the time they need. If you anticipate needing extended time off, discuss with your manager or reach out to Kenna Meyerhoff.

I coordinate clients. What do I hand over before I go?

The Coordinator OOO template is a ready-made structure for going out of office as a coordinator. Duplicate it, rename it to “[Your Name] OOO”, fill in the placeholders, and share with your coverage team and Carrara POCs at least one week before leaving.

What it covers

  • Basic info: dates, availability while out, emergency contact.
  • A coverage table per client: who covers, primary and secondary, and the scope.
  • Per-client breakdown: POCs, open roles, active candidates, quirks.
  • Recurring tasks and meetings that need covering.
  • Known risks, escalation order, and your communication plan (auto-reply, Slack status).
  • A return plan and a pre-departure checklist (confirm with Hayley, add to calendars, post in #g-ooo).

Ask your team lead for the current template document to duplicate, or read the full page in Notion.
