How to read our Slack.

Prefixes tell you what a channel is for. Once you know the pattern, the workspace reads itself.

g-: company-wide, everyone's in.
f-: function teams (talent, GTM, ops).
c-: one channel per client.
p-: projects within an account.
ext-: shared with the client. Watch what you write.
temp-: sprints, archived after.

Set up your profile properly

Your Slack profile is how the rest of the company finds you, and the team page in this hub pulls straight from it. Fill in all of it on day one.

Photo: a real photo of your face, not a logo or an avatar.
Full name: the name people will search for.
Title: your role and the clients you sit on.
Email: your carrara.is address.
Where you're based: city and country, so people know your hours.
Timezone: set it correctly. We work across four continents.

Phone number is optional, add it if you're happy being reached that way.
