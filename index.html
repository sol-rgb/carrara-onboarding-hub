How to read our Slack.

Prefixes tell you what a channel is for. Once you know the pattern, the workspace reads itself.

g-: company-wide, everyone's in.
f-: function teams (talent, GTM, ops).
c-: one channel per client.
p-: projects within an account.
ext-: shared with the client. Watch what you write.
temp-: sprints, archived after.
